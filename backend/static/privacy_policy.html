2 Information We Collect

Lazy Monkey collects and processes specific categories of data necessary to deliver seamless cashier billing, atomic inventory tracking, and merchant operational analytics. The data collected includes:

  • Merchant & Account Profile Data: Business name, registered trade address, GSTIN / Tax Identification Numbers, owner contact phone number, billing email address, cashier full names, and access control credentials.
  • Inventory & Product Data: Product catalog listings, Stock Keeping Units (SKUs), barcodes, Universal Product Codes (UPC), cost prices, Maximum Retail Price (MRP), selling prices, tax rates, reorder thresholds, and category classifications.
  • Transaction & Register Logs: Daily register sales, itemized cart details, discounts applied, cash drawer balances, shift opening/closing totals, order timestamps, invoice numbers, and payment method categories (Cash, Card, UPI, Digital Wallet).
  • Customer Relationship Management (CRM) Data: Customer contact names, phone numbers, transaction frequency, loyalty point balances, and store credit logs added directly by the merchant.
  • Technical & Peripheral Diagnostics: Terminal IP address, browser user agents, mobile device IDs, Bluetooth ESC/POS thermal printer pairing diagnostics, barcode scanner inputs, and system error traces.

3 How We Use Collected Data

We utilize the collected information strictly for operational, security, and computational purposes related to store management:

  • To process real-time Point of Sale checkouts, calculate atomic stock deductions, and print dynamic thermal sales receipts.
  • To synchronize product catalog updates and inventory counts across multiple store registers and cloud database clusters.
  • To authenticate cashiers, enforce Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and maintain audit trails for register shifts.
  • To calculate daily sales summaries, profit margins, top-selling product metrics, and financial reporting insights.
  • To maintain system uptime, detect unauthorized access attempts, and provide customer support.

Lazy Monkey grants the merchant a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to access and utilize the platform during the active subscription period.

5 Security Architecture & Data Protection

Lazy Monkey implements multi-layered security safeguards to protect merchant databases against unauthorized disclosure, alteration, or destruction:

  • Encryption in Transit & Rest: All web and mobile communications with Lazy Monkey cloud API endpoints are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Sensitive credentials and API tokens are hashed using enterprise cryptographic standards (bcrypt/HS256).
  • Tenant Isolation: Merchant data is segmented using strict database tenant scoping identifiers, ensuring your store data cannot be accessed by unauthorized third parties or other store accounts.
  • Automated Backups: System databases undergo continuous transactional logging and daily encrypted cloud backups to protect against hardware failures or data loss.

6 Hardware & Third-Party Integrations

Lazy Monkey interfaces with hardware peripherals (such as Bluetooth ESC/POS thermal receipt printers, USB barcode scanners, and cash drawers) and third-party utility APIs (such as SMS/Email gateways for digital receipt delivery). Lazy Monkey is not liable for hardware malfunctions, printer hardware firmware defects, or third-party network outages beyond our reasonable control.

Merchants retain the right to export their product catalogs, customer databases, and sales reports in standard CSV or PDF formats at any time. Upon merchant account termination, stored transactional data is retained for 30 days to facilitate final data extraction before permanent deletion from active database nodes.

9 Mobile Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

The LazyMonkeyAI mobile application requests specific device permissions strictly to enable essential Point-of-Sale hardware functionality:

  • Bluetooth & Nearby Devices (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN): Required solely to discover, pair, and transmit receipt print data to wireless ESC/POS thermal printers.
  • Camera Access (CAMERA): Used exclusively for real-time barcode scanning of product items during inventory management and cashier checkout.
  • Location Access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Mandatory under Android OS guidelines for Bluetooth LE hardware device discovery. Location coordinates are never recorded, tracked, or transmitted.
