6. ARE INSPECTION IMAGES SAVED?

6.1 Images Submitted Through the App

When an authorized user intentionally captures or selects an image and submits it as part of an inspection or work-related activity, the image may be transmitted to the application's authorized backend/server infrastructure.

Once successfully submitted, the image may be stored as part of the relevant organizational record.

The image may therefore remain available after the user closes the App or removes the image from the device.

6.2 Why Images May Be Stored

Images may be stored to:

Maintain an inspection record.

Verify inspection activities.

Support audit requirements.

Review inspection findings.

Allow authorized supervisors to verify submissions.

Generate inspection reports.

Compare current and previous inspection conditions.

Support follow-up actions.

Maintain organizational records.

Investigate disputes or fraudulent submissions.

6.3 Images Are Not Automatically Stored Merely Because the Camera Opens

Opening or using the camera does not by itself mean that every photograph taken with the device becomes part of the organization's database.

Images are intended to become an organizational record when the user captures/selects and submits them through the relevant App functionality.

7. ARE IMAGES SAVED TO THE USER'S PHONE GALLERY?

The App's handling of device photographs depends on the operating system and implementation of the specific feature.

Where the App captures an image directly through its camera interface, the image may be temporarily processed by the App for submission.

The App does not intentionally copy every inspection photograph into the user's personal photo gallery unless such functionality is explicitly implemented.

Where a user selects an existing photograph through the device's photo picker or file-selection mechanism, the App accesses the selected item for the requested operation rather than obtaining unrestricted access to the user's entire personal photo library.

The App does not intentionally scan or collect unrelated personal photographs.

No.

The App's intended use of photographs is limited to legitimate application functionality.

The App does not intentionally:

Scan the user's complete personal gallery.

Build a personal photo database.

Collect unrelated photographs.

Upload unrelated personal photographs.
